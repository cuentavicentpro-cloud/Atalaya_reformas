## Brand & Style

This design system translates the architectural precision and Mediterranean authenticity of high-end home transformations into a serene, tactile digital experience. The brand voice balances architectural authority with warm hospitality: confident, meticulous, rooted in place, yet distinctly modern.

### Emotional Tone & Target Audience
- **Target Audience:** Discerning homeowners, property investors, and interior design enthusiasts seeking comprehensive renovation, architectural rehabilitation, and turn-key interior craftsmanship across Murcia and the Costa Cálida.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Calm assurance, unhurried craftsmanship, spatial clarity, and material warmth. The interface feels less like a generic contractor listing and more like a private consultation inside a sunlit architectural studio.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
- **Warm Minimalist Mediterranean:** Influenced by contemporary Iberian architecture, artisanal stone, clay finishes, and generous natural lighting.
- **Precision Structure:** Drawing directly from isometric axonometric logic (echoed in the brand mark), layouts feature disciplined structural geometry balanced with organic earthen textures, generous breathing space, and subtle physical boundaries.

## Colors

The color palette reflects natural Mediterranean construction materials: fired terracotta, olive foliage, dry limestone, and sun-bleached linen, anchored by refined charcoal ink for typography.

### Role Assignments & Surface Strategy
- **Primary (`#B8502F` - Terracota Cálido):** Serves as the active call-to-action color, hero emphasis, highlight indicators, and active status anchors. Never used for massive background slabs; reserved for focal points and deliberate user interactions.
- **Secondary (`#5F6F52` - Verde Oliva / Salvia):** Conveys balance, eco-conscious materials, verified craftsmanship badges, and secondary micro-interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A373` - Albero Arena):** Used for subtle category chips, accent borders, testimonial quotes, and architectural drawing tints.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - `Surface Base`: `#FBF9F5` (Caliza Lino) — The primary canvas tone; soft on the eyes, warm, avoiding sterile digital white.
  - `Surface Elevated / Container`: `#FFFFFF` — Used within cards, modal dialogs, and elevated sheets to establish clean contrast against `#FBF9F5`.
  - `Surface Subdued`: `#F5F0EB` (Arena Suave) — Used for secondary container sections, input backgrounds, and table zebra striping.
  - `Border / Outline`: `#E8E2DA` (Piedra Fina) — Low-contrast structural borders that define geometry without visual harshness.
  - `Text Primary`: `#1C1A18` (Carbón Mineral) — Deep charcoal providing AAA contrast against lino backgrounds while avoiding the harshness of pure black.
  - `Text Muted`: `#68635C` (Ceniza Cálida) — Captions, architectural specs, and secondary metadata.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** across display, body, and label roles. Its geometric precision evokes architectural drafting, while its humanist apertures maintain Mediterranean warmth and immediate legibility.

### Hierarchy & Editorial Flourishes
- **Display & Large Headlines:** Set with tight tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.02em`) and strong line-height control to create structured, architectural title treatments.
- **Architectural Project Details / Subheadings:** Uppercase styling combined with `label-md` or `label-sm` with expanded letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) is applied to metadata badges, phase tags (e.g., *ESTRUCTURA*, *ACABADOS*, *ILUMINACIÓN*), and floor area numbers.
- **Body & Long Form:** Preserves high airiness with a 1.6x leading ratio in `body-md` and `body-lg` to guarantee effortless reading of project scopes, materials catalogs, and budget estimates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a proportional 12-column architectural grid with generous margins, echoing the open floor plans and courtyards of Mediterranean modern homes.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12-column fluid grid, max-width `1360px`, `margin-lg` (4rem / 64px), `gutter-lg` (2rem / 32px). Generous negative space balances heavy architectural photography.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid, `margin` (1.5rem / 24px), `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Hero imagery transitions to stacked ratios.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4-column grid, `margin-sm` (1rem / 16px), `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px). Cards span 4 columns with edge bleed options for project photo carousels.

### Spacing Cadence
A base unit of `8px` (`0.5rem`) governs all internal element paddings and rhythm. Section transitions use `space-xl` multiplied for rhythm, ensuring content feels uncrowded and gallery-like.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is conveyed through warm ambient shadows and tonal surface layering, evoking southern sunlight casting soft architectural shadows onto stucco and limestone.

### Ambient Warm Shadows
Shadows avoid cold, synthetic grayscale blurs. They incorporate warm charcoal with a subtle amber undertone:
- **Low (Default Cards & Input Fields):** `0px 2px 8px -1px rgba(28, 26, 24, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(28, 26, 24, 0.02)`.
- **Medium (Hovered Cards & Dropdowns):** `0px 12px 24px -4px rgba(28, 26, 24, 0.07), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(28, 26, 24, 0.03)`.
- **High (Floating Budget Estimator & Modals):** `0px 24px 48px -8px rgba(28, 26, 24, 0.12), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(28, 26, 24, 0.04)`.

### Tonal Stratification & Borders
- Flat layers use `#E8E2DA` fine 1px strokes rather than heavy box shadows to delineate content zones cleanly.
- Backdrop glassmorphism is restricted to sticky navigation headers: `background: rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border-bottom: 1px solid #E8E2DA`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined radius scale (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting the smooth bullnose corners, plastered archways, and sculpted carpentry found in contemporary Mediterranean renovation projects.

### Corner Curvature Rules
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `0.5rem` (8px) for balanced, tactile touch targets.
- **Structural Containers (Project Cards, Content Slices):** `1rem` (16px) via `rounded-lg`, softening large visual masses while maintaining crisp structural alignment.
- **Hero Focus Cards & Modals:** `1.5rem` (24px) via `rounded-xl`.
- **Axonometric Accents:** Pure geometric shapes (triangles and parallelograms derived from the brand logo) may appear in decorative line watermarks or step indicators without rounded corners to maintain architectural precision.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Terracota (`#B8502F`), text in white (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, radius `0.5rem`. Hover state shifts gently to `#9E4326` with subtle upward transform (`translateY(-1px)`) and medium shadow.
- **Secondary / Outline:** 1.5px border in `#1C1A18`, background transparent, text `#1C1A18`. Hover fills with `#F5F0EB`.
- **Subtle / Text Button:** Text in Terracota `#B8502F` with an animated right arrow icon shifting 4px on hover.

### Project & Service Cards
- Built on a crisp white container (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a 1px border (`#E8E2DA`) with `rounded-lg` (16px).
- Features full-width photography at 4:3 or 16:9 ratio with an internal 12px padding gap from the card perimeter for an editorial passe-partout frame.
- Includes category pill, renovation scope summary, and location tag (*ej. "Gran Vía, Murcia"*).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Small padding (`0.25rem 0.75rem`), radius `0.25rem`, uppercase `label-sm`.
- **Olive Chip (`#5F6F52` at 12% opacity background, solid `#3F4A37` text):** Indicates sustainable finishes, turnkey completion, or active permits.
- **Terracotta Chip (`#B8502F` at 10% opacity background, solid `#B8502F` text):** Highlights premium features, before/after showcases, and architectural consultations.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- Background `#F5F0EB` transitioning to pure `#FFFFFF` upon focus.
- Border 1px `#E8E2DA`, transitioning to 1.5px solid `#B8502F` upon focus with zero jarring outer glow rings; uses a subtle warm focus ring (`rgba(184, 80, 47, 0.15)`).
- Labels positioned above inputs in `#1C1A18`, medium weight with secondary helper texts in `#68635C`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Custom square (`0.25rem` radius) or circle controls with a 1.5px `#68635C` outline.
- Selected state fills with Terracota `#B8502F` featuring a crisp white geometric checkmark or central dot.

### Specialized Architectural Components
- **Before / After Slider:** A two-layer split-screen image container with a vertical drag handle in Terracota `#B8502F` accented with an off-white dual directional arrow.
- **Renovation Quote Estimator Widget:** Step-by-step interactive card container with tactile material selector tiles (e.g., *Cocina integral*, *Baño suite*, *Reforma integral*), displaying live calculated estimates with a breakdown of phases and high-fidelity specifications.
